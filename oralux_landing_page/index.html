Services Offered

We provide a wide range of quality dental treatments tailored to your needs.

Dental Consultation

Comprehensive oral checkup and expert evaluation for your dental health.

Oral Prophylaxis

Professional tooth cleaning to remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains.

Restoration (Pasta)

High-quality fillings to repair decayed, damaged, or fractured teeth.

Extraction (Bunot)

Safe and gentle tooth removal for severely damaged or problematic teeth.

Denture (Pustiso)

Custom removable tooth replacements to restore function and your natural smile.

Orthodontics (Braces)

Alignment treatments using modern dental braces to straighten crooked teeth.

Odontectomy

Surgical extraction specialized for impacted wisdom tooth removal.

Teeth Whitening

Professional brightening treatment to give you a visibly whiter and radiant smile.

Veneers

Custom shell restorations placed over teeth to correct shape, color, and minor gaps.

Dental Crown / Fixed Bridge

Durable caps and bridges to cover damaged teeth or fill gaps between missing teeth.

Root Canal Treatment

Endodontic care to save infected or severely damaged inner tooth pulp.
